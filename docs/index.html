Puxi

An 8-track drum step sequencer for Ableton Live 12, built for deep Live and Push 3 integration.

Download View on GitHub
Free & open source · requires Ableton Live 12 (Max for Live)

What it does

Multi-track grid

All 8 drum tracks at once, 64 steps, with Drum Rack names and colors.

Probability

Per-step chance to play, plus a non-destructive global offset.

Length & ties

Staccato to multi-step ties that spill over into the next pages.

Ratcheting

2–8 evenly-spaced sub-hits per step, a roll on the grid.

Polyrhythm

Per-track loop lengths with independent playheads.

Groove & swing

Follows Live's Groove and Swing amounts, live and on export.

Parameter locks

Lock two of a drum pad's device params per step, Elektron-style.

Full Push 3

Pads, encoders, and buttons — tethered and standalone.

Get started

  1. Download Puxi.amxd, move it into your Live User Library, and drag it onto a MIDI track.
  2. Add a Drum Rack after Puxi on the same track.
  3. Hit play — and reach for Push 3 if you have one.

FAQ

What do I need?

Ableton Live 12 with Max for Live (included in Suite, an add-on for Standard). Puxi is a single .amxd.

Do I need a Push?

No — the mouse GUI does everything. A Push 3 or Push 2 adds hands-on pads, encoders and buttons.

Push 3 standalone?

Yes. Transfer the device to the Push and it runs without a computer. Push 2 works too (tethered).

How much?

Free. No pro version, no unlock, no email wall. Open source under GPLv3.

Was it made with AI?

Yes. Puxi was designed by a producer and tested in Live and on a Push 3; Claude Code, an AI coding assistant, wrote most of the code. How Puxi was made →
